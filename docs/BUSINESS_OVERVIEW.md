# Business Overview: AI SEC Filing Analyzer

## Executive Summary

The AI SEC Filing Analyzer transforms how financial professionals interact with regulatory documents by enabling **natural language queries** against complex SEC filings. Instead of manually searching through hundreds of pages, users simply ask questions like *"What were Q3 2024 earnings?"* and receive accurate, sourced answers in seconds.

## Solution Overview

### What It Does
The system allows users to upload SEC filing URLs and ask natural language questions. The AI processes the document, finds relevant sections, and provides comprehensive answers with source citations.

### How It Works (Simplified)
1. **Document Processing**: System fetches and prepares SEC filing for analysis
2. **Smart Indexing**: Content is organized for rapid, contextual search
3. **Question Processing**: User questions are analyzed to understand intent
4. **Intelligent Retrieval**: System finds relevant document sections
5. **AI Analysis**: Advanced AI generates accurate, contextual responses
6. **Source Attribution**: All answers include references to original document sections
